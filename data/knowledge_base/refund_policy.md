# Fee Reversal, Refund & Transaction Dispute Policy

**Document ID:** KB-REF-2026-03
**Owner:** Deposit Operations & Claims Governance
**Applies to:** Northgate Bank Customer Care (Tier 1 & Tier 2), Digital Servicing
**Effective:** 2026-03-01
**Last reviewed:** 2026-06-18
**Review cycle:** Semi-annual
**Classification:** Internal — Approved for agent-assist retrieval

> **Scope note.** This document covers fee reversals and refunds on consumer deposit
> accounts, and disputes of debit card, ACH, Zelle® and wire transactions. It does
> **not** cover credit card chargeback processing, mortgage or home equity escrow
> adjustments, auto and personal loan payoff disputes, brokerage or IRA transfers,
> or commercial/treasury services. If a customer's request falls outside the scope
> above, the agent must state that the applicable policy could not be verified and
> escalate.

---

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Courtesy reversal** | A discretionary refund of a correctly assessed fee, offered as a one-time gesture of goodwill. |
| **Error reversal** | Refund of a fee assessed in error by the Bank. Not discretionary and not counted against courtesy limits. |
| **Unauthorized transfer** | An electronic funds transfer from a consumer account initiated by a person other than the consumer without actual authority. Governed by Regulation E. |
| **Merchant dispute** | A transaction the customer authorized but is contesting on quality, delivery, amount or duplication grounds. Governed by card network rules, not Regulation E. |
| **Provisional credit** | A temporary credit issued to the customer's account while a Regulation E claim is investigated. |
| **Account in good standing** | Open, not overdrawn beyond the Bank's overdraft limit, no charge-off history in 24 months, and not subject to a legal hold or restraint. |

## 2. Fee reversals (FEE)

### FEE-001 — One-time courtesy reversal
A customer is eligible for **one courtesy fee reversal per rolling 12-month period**
when **all** of the following are true:

1. The fee posted within the **last 60 calendar days**.
2. The fee type is eligible: Overdraft Fee, Non-Sufficient Funds (Returned Item) Fee,
   Monthly Service Fee, Excess Withdrawal Fee, or Stop Payment Fee.
3. The account is **in good standing** (see Definitions).
4. The customer has **no prior courtesy reversal in the last 12 months**.

Eligible amount: up to **$140** total per occurrence (equivalent to four overdraft
fees at $35). Reversals post to the account within **1–2 business days**.

Agents may confirm eligibility and complete this reversal without approval.

### FEE-002 — Repeat reversal requests
If the customer has **already received a courtesy reversal within the last 12 months**,
the request must be **escalated to the Service Recovery team**. Tier 1 agents must not
promise or deny a second reversal. Service Recovery reviews relationship value,
overdraft frequency and any hardship indicators before deciding.

### FEE-003 — Fees on newly opened accounts
Fees assessed on accounts open **fewer than 30 days**, and any dispute involving an
**account-opening promotional offer** (bonus not received, promotional fee waiver not
applied), must be escalated to the New Account Servicing queue. These cases require
review of the original offer terms, which Tier 1 agents cannot access.

### FEE-004 — Monthly Service Fee waiver criteria
The $12 Monthly Service Fee on Everyday Checking is **automatically waived** for a
statement cycle when **any one** of the following is met:

- Total qualifying direct deposits of **$500 or more** post during the cycle; **or**
- **Minimum daily balance of $1,500** is maintained throughout the cycle; **or**
- The primary account owner is **age 17–24** and enrolled in Campus Banking; **or**
- The account is linked to a Northgate Premier relationship (combined balances $25,000+).

If a customer met a waiver condition and the fee still posted, treat as an
**error reversal** (not a courtesy reversal) and process without counting it toward
FEE-001. If the customer did **not** meet any condition, explain the criteria and the
options available; the fee itself is correctly assessed.

### FEE-005 — Out-of-network ATM fee reimbursement
- **Everyday Checking:** no reimbursement. The $3.00 Northgate fee plus any operator
  surcharge is correctly assessed.
- **Premier Checking:** up to **2 reimbursements per statement cycle**, applied
  automatically. If a qualifying reimbursement did not apply, process as an error
  reversal.
- **International ATM withdrawals** additionally carry a 3% foreign transaction fee,
  which is **not reimbursable** on any tier.

### FEE-006 — Fees older than 60 days
Requests to reverse a fee that posted **more than 60 days ago** are outside Tier 1
authority. Escalate to Service Recovery with the fee date and amount. Do not tell the
customer the fee is permanently non-refundable.

### FEE-007 — Wire transfer and cashier's check fees
Wire fees ($30 domestic outgoing, $45 international outgoing, $15 incoming) and
cashier's check fees ($10) are **not eligible for courtesy reversal**. Exceptions
require Wire Operations approval — escalate.

## 3. Electronic transaction disputes (DSP)

### DSP-001 — Unauthorized transactions (Regulation E)
Applies when the customer states they **did not authorize** a debit card transaction,
ACH debit or electronic withdrawal.

**Reporting window:** the customer must report the unauthorized transfer within
**60 calendar days of the statement date** on which it first appeared. Claims reported
after 60 days may be denied for the amounts appearing on that statement — do not state
this as a final outcome; escalate for review.

**Investigation timeline:**

| Milestone | Standard timeline |
| --- | --- |
| Claim acknowledged | Same business day |
| Investigation completed | **10 business days** |
| Provisional credit issued if not resolved by day 10 | By end of **day 10** |
| Extended investigation | Up to **45 calendar days** |
| Extended investigation — POS, foreign-initiated, or accounts open < 30 days | Up to **90 calendar days** |
| Written result notice after resolution | Within **3 business days** |

**Required at intake:** transaction date, amount, merchant name as shown, whether the
card is in the customer's possession, whether anyone else had access to the card or
credentials, and whether the customer has filed a police report (optional, not required).

**Card handling:** if the physical card is compromised, block and reissue immediately
under TRB-010 in the troubleshooting FAQ. A dispute may proceed without card
replacement if the customer confirms the card is in their possession and the
transaction was card-not-present.

### DSP-002 — Merchant and billing disputes
Applies when the customer **authorized** the transaction but contests it: goods or
services not received, not as described, duplicate charge, incorrect amount,
cancelled subscription still billing, or a free trial converted without notice.

- Filing window: **120 calendar days from the transaction date** (or from the expected
  delivery date, where later, up to a 540-day outer limit).
- The customer must first attempt to resolve directly with the merchant, **except**
  where the merchant is unreachable, out of business, or the customer reports the
  merchant refused. Document the attempt or the reason it was skipped.
- Provisional credit is **not automatic** for merchant disputes. Do not promise credit.
- Recurring subscription billing: the customer should also revoke authorization with
  the merchant (see the subscription policy, SUB-005) or the debits may continue.

### DSP-003 — Claims requiring specialist review
Escalate to a Claims Specialist, without stating a likely outcome, when **any** apply:

- Disputed amount **exceeds $2,500** (single transaction or aggregate on one claim).
- The customer has filed **3 or more disputes in the trailing 12 months**.
- The claim involves a **prior denied claim being re-opened**.
- The customer names a **family member, household member, caregiver or former partner**
  as the person who made the transaction. (Do not characterize this as "not fraud" —
  these claims require elder-abuse and coercion screening.)
- The account is a **business or fiduciary** account (Reg E consumer protections do not
  apply the same way).
- The customer reports **identity theft** or an unrecognized account opened in their name.

### DSP-004 — Zelle® and person-to-person transfers
- **Unauthorized** Zelle transfers (someone accessed the customer's account and sent
  funds) are treated under **DSP-001**.
- **Authorized** transfers the customer now regrets — including transfers sent to the
  wrong phone number or email, and transfers to a person who did not deliver goods —
  are **generally not recoverable**. The Bank may attempt a **recall request** to the
  receiving institution, which is **not guaranteed and typically fails once funds are
  withdrawn**.
- **Scam claims** (the customer was deceived into sending, e.g. impostor, romance,
  invoice, "bank fraud department" scams) must be **escalated to the Fraud
  Investigations team**. Certain imposter scam claims may be reimbursable under
  network rules; Tier 1 agents must not assess eligibility.
- Never tell a customer that a Zelle transfer "cannot be reversed, period." Use:
  a recall will be attempted, and the claim will be reviewed.

### DSP-005 — Wire transfers
Consumer wire transfers are **not covered by Regulation E**. There is no dispute right
for an executed wire. The only remedy is a **recall request** to the beneficiary bank,
which requires beneficiary consent. All wire recall requests — domestic or
international — must be **escalated to Wire Operations the same business day**.
Time is critical; do not hold these for follow-up. Remittance transfers to consumers
abroad may carry a 30-minute cancellation right under Regulation E, subpart B; Wire
Operations determines applicability.

### DSP-006 — Statements agents must not make
Do not, in any drafted reply:

- Confirm, deny or predict the **outcome** of an open investigation.
- State that a **credit is guaranteed**, or give a credit date before the claim is filed.
- Disclose **fraud detection rules, scoring thresholds, or velocity limits**.
- Blame the customer for the loss, or state that they "authorized it so nothing can be done."
- Reference another customer's or a third party's account activity.
- Provide a case number that has not been generated by the claims system.

### DSP-007 — Duplicate posting vs. authorization hold
Before opening a dispute for a "double charge," confirm whether one entry is still
**pending**. Pending authorization holds (common with fuel pumps, hotels, car rentals
and restaurants) drop off within **3 business days** and are not duplicates. Only open a
DSP-002 claim when **both entries have posted**. See TRB-007 and TRB-009.

## 4. Refunds of principal (non-fee)

| Situation | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit posted to the wrong Northgate account | Error correction — escalate to Deposit Operations, same day |
| Duplicate mobile deposit of the same check | Escalate to Deposit Operations; one entry will be reversed |
| Customer requests reversal of their own completed internal transfer | Self-service is unavailable once posted; agent may move funds back with customer confirmation if both accounts are the customer's |
| Overdraft caused by a Bank posting error | Error reversal of all resulting fees; does not count against FEE-001 |
| Interest or APY not credited as advertised | Escalate to Deposit Operations with the offer reference |

## 5. Decision quick reference

| Customer situation | Route | Cite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First overdraft fee, posted 9 days ago, account current | Auto-resolve — process reversal | FEE-001 |
| Second fee reversal request in 5 months | Escalate — Service Recovery | FEE-002 |
| Monthly fee posted but $2,100 average balance held all cycle | Auto-resolve — error reversal | FEE-004 |
| Fee posted 4 months ago | Escalate | FEE-006 |
| "I don't recognize this $89 charge" — card in hand | Auto-resolve intake, file claim | DSP-001 |
| Unrecognized charges totaling $4,300 | Escalate — Claims Specialist | DSP-003 |
| Merchant never shipped, customer emailed them twice | Auto-resolve intake, file claim | DSP-002 |
| Merchant dispute, no merchant contact attempted, merchant reachable | Ask for more information | DSP-002 |
| Zelle sent to wrong number | Escalate — recall attempt | DSP-004 |
| Zelle sent after a phone call from "the fraud department" | Escalate — Fraud Investigations | DSP-004 |
| Wire sent to a fraudulent seller | Escalate — Wire Operations, same day | DSP-005 |
| Mortgage escrow overage refund | **No policy in scope** — state as unverified and escalate | Scope note |
