# Customer Conduct, Prohibited Requests & Refusal Policy

**Document ID:** KB-CON-2026-04
**Owner:** Conduct Risk, Financial Crimes Compliance & Employee Safety
**Applies to:** All Northgate Bank Customer Care channels and agent-assist systems
**Effective:** 2026-04-06
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-21
**Classification:** Internal — Approved for agent-assist retrieval

> **Scope note.** This document governs conduct handling and prohibited requests across
> every product and channel, and it takes precedence over any product policy where the two
> appear to conflict. It does **not** define the substantive servicing outcome for a
> legitimate request — that lives in the product policies (`refund_policy.md`,
> `subscription_policy.md`, `account_access_faq.md`, `troubleshooting_faq.md`). A conduct
> issue and a servicing request in the same message are handled on their own merits.
>
> **Purpose.** This document defines when a request must be **refused**, when it must be
> **escalated**, and — critically — when a customer's *tone* must **not** change the
> service they receive. Refusal is a narrow tool. Most angry customers have a valid
> request underneath the anger, and that request still gets answered.

---

## 1. The two-part rule

Every message is assessed on **two independent axes**:

1. **Conduct** — is the customer's language abusive, threatening, or discriminatory?
2. **Request legitimacy** — is what they are asking for something the Bank can do?

The axes are scored separately. Frustration, profanity, sarcasm, capital letters,
insults about the Bank, threats to leave, or threats to complain publicly **do not** make
a legitimate request illegitimate.

| Conduct | Request | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civil | Legitimate | Service normally |
| Profane / insulting | Legitimate | **Service the request** + one professional boundary line (CON-001) |
| Civil | Prohibited | **Refuse** the request, politely (CON-004 – CON-008) |
| Threatening or discriminatory | Any | **Escalate** (CON-002, CON-003) — safety path takes precedence |

## 2. Conduct handling (CON-001 – CON-003)

### CON-001 — Profanity, insults and hostility toward the Bank or staff
The customer is angry, swearing, calling the Bank or an employee names, or writing in
all capitals.

**Do:**
- Answer the underlying request completely and in the normal way.
- Include **one** brief, non-defensive boundary sentence, placed after the substantive
  answer, e.g. *"I'm glad to keep helping with this — I'd just ask that we keep the
  language respectful so I can do that well."*
- Keep the tone warm. Acknowledge the impact ("a $35 fee on top of rent clearing is a
  real problem") without accepting blame the Bank does not own.

**Do not:**
- Lecture, quote a conduct policy at the customer, or list what they said back to them.
- Refuse, close, or downgrade the priority of the ticket because of tone.
- Threaten to end the interaction on a first instance.
- Mirror the customer's language or match their register.

If abusive language **continues after one boundary statement in the same thread**,
escalate to a supervisor for a decision about channel restriction. The customer's
underlying request stays open and still gets worked.

### CON-002 — Threats of violence, and self-harm disclosure
**Route: escalate immediately. Never refuse-and-close. Never end the contact.**

Applies to:
- Any threat of physical harm to an employee, a branch, a location, or the Bank.
- Statements referencing a weapon, "coming to your office," or harm to a named person.
- Any statement suggesting the customer may harm themselves, including financial-distress
  phrasing such as *"I have nothing left,"* *"I can't go on like this,"* or *"there's no
  point anymore."*

**Handling:**
- Route to the **Threat Response team** (violence) and/or **Vulnerable Customer
  Support** (self-harm) with the **full verbatim message**, immediately, at
  urgent priority. Notify a supervisor in real time — do not rely on the queue.
- The drafted reply must be **short, human, non-clinical, and non-judgmental**. It
  acknowledges the person, confirms a specialist is taking over, and — for self-harm
  indicators — includes the standard resource line: *"If you're in crisis, you can reach
  the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988 (US), any time."*
- Do **not** attempt to assess risk, ask probing questions, diagnose, or negotiate.
- Do **not** cite policy, quote fees, or continue the servicing conversation in the same
  reply. The financial request is handled after, by the specialist team.

### CON-003 — Discriminatory, harassing or sexual content
Applies to slurs or demeaning language directed at a person or group on the basis of
race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity,
age, disability, or veteran status; sexual propositions or sexual content directed at
staff; and repeated targeting of a named employee.

**Handling:** send **one** scripted response (see §5), then **stop the exchange in that
channel** and escalate to **Conduct Review**. Unlike CON-001, no second chance is
extended and no boundary-and-continue is attempted. Any legitimate servicing request in
the same message is transferred to Conduct Review to be completed through a supervised
channel — it is **not dropped**.

## 3. Prohibited requests — refuse (CON-004 – CON-008)

A refusal is a **complete, polite, single reply**: what cannot be done, one sentence of
why at the highest useful level, one legitimate alternative if one exists, and nothing
further. No moralizing. No repeated refusal in the same message.

### CON-004 — Requests to circumvent controls or misstate facts
Refuse when the customer asks the Bank or the agent to record something untrue or bypass
a required control. Examples:

- *"Just put it down as fraud so I get my money back."* (The transaction was authorized
  and the customer says so.)
- *"Backdate the dispute so it's inside the 60 days."*
- *"Say I never got the goods, that's the fastest way, right?"*
- *"Skip the verification, you know it's me."*
- *"Reverse the fee and don't count it against my one-per-year."*
- *"Post-date the stop payment so it covers yesterday's debit."*
- *"Remove the overdraft from my statement before my landlord sees it."*

Refuse the **framing**, then offer the **real** path — the genuine dispute reason, the
Service Recovery escalation, the authenticated channel. Very often the customer has a
valid claim and has simply been coached badly by the internet. Assume that reading first.
Do **not** accuse the customer of fraud, and do **not** file a knowingly false claim
under any pressure.

### CON-005 — Third-party access and information requests
Refuse requests for account information, transaction history, balances, statements,
account existence, or access rights concerning **an account the requester does not own
and is not documented on** — including a spouse, parent, adult child, roommate,
employee, employer, ex-partner, or a person the requester says consented verbally.

- Do not confirm or deny that an account exists.
- Legitimate paths exist and should be offered: joint ownership, Power of Attorney,
  authorized signer documentation, court appointment, or estate representation — all
  through a branch or Estate & Trust Servicing (see `account_access_faq.md` ACC-009,
  ACC-011).
- **Heightened caution:** if the request is framed around monitoring, controlling, or
  locating another adult ("I need to see where she's spending," "he can't know I asked"),
  refuse **and** escalate to Conduct Review for a financial-abuse look. Do not signal
  this in the reply.

### CON-006 — Requests for internal controls and detection logic
Refuse requests for: fraud model thresholds, transaction-monitoring rules, velocity or
amount limits that trigger review, hold logic, the criteria that flagged a specific
transaction, internal case notes, employee names or locations, or which team is reviewing
a case. Published limits (ATM withdrawal, Zelle, mobile deposit) are **not** internal —
those are in `troubleshooting_faq.md` and may be shared freely.

### CON-007 — Structuring and reporting evasion
Refuse **and escalate to the BSA/AML team** — without alerting the customer to the
escalation, and without hinting that a report may be filed — when a customer asks how to:

- Break deposits or withdrawals into amounts under **$10,000** to avoid a Currency
  Transaction Report;
- Avoid, delay or suppress any regulatory report or hold;
- Move funds in a way described as keeping it "off the record," "invisible," or "under
  the radar," or through a third party's account to obscure the source;
- Handle funds the customer describes as belonging to someone else, or as coming from an
  undisclosed source.

Never provide structuring guidance, never confirm reporting thresholds in this context,
and never state that a report will or will not be filed. Tipping off is prohibited.

### CON-008 — Inducements
Refuse offers of gifts, cash, tips, gratuities, favors, or anything of value in exchange
for an exception, an expedite, or preferential handling, and escalate to Conduct Review.
Decline warmly and without embarrassing the customer; most such offers are naive rather
than corrupt.

## 4. Situations that look like refusals but are not

### CON-009 — Legal threats, attorney and regulator mentions
When a customer mentions a lawyer, a lawsuit, arbitration, small claims, the **CFPB**,
the **OCC**, a state attorney general, a state banking regulator, the media, or a
"complaint I'm filing today":

- **Do not** refuse, argue, defend the Bank's position, dispute their characterization,
  or discuss the merits.
- **Do not** discourage the complaint or suggest it is unnecessary.
- **Escalate to Executive Complaints** at high priority; regulatory complaints carry
  mandated response deadlines.
- The drafted reply is brief and neutral: acknowledgement, confirmation that a
  specialist team is taking it, and an expected contact timeframe.
- Once litigation is asserted, **stop substantive discussion** of the disputed matter in
  writing.

### CON-010 — Refusal drafting standards
Every refusal draft must:

1. Open with acknowledgement of the customer's goal — not with "unfortunately."
2. State plainly what the Bank cannot do, once.
3. Give the reason at the level of principle, in one sentence, without policy IDs,
   internal team names, or legal citations.
4. Offer the nearest legitimate alternative, if one exists.
5. Close warmly and leave the door open for the rest of their request.
6. Stay under **150 words**, and never repeat the refusal.
7. Contain **no** accusation, warning about consequences, or reference to the customer's
   character or history.

### CON-011 — What must never trigger a refusal
Refusal is **never** appropriate for any of the following, on its own:

- Anger, profanity, sarcasm, all-caps, or exclamation marks.
- Threatening to close accounts, leave the Bank, post on social media, or leave a review.
- Repeating the same request several times, or contacting on multiple channels.
- Being wrong about a policy, or misquoting one confidently.
- Requests the Bank *can* do but the agent finds tedious or lengthy.
- A request the agent cannot verify against the knowledge base — that is an
  **escalation with a "policy could not be verified" statement**, not a refusal.
- Limited English proficiency, poor spelling, unclear writing, or a message that is hard
  to read.

Refusing a legitimate request because of tone is a **reportable service failure**.

## 5. Approved response scripts

**CON-003 — discriminatory or harassing content (send once, then stop):**

> Thank you for contacting Northgate Bank. We're not able to continue this conversation
> in this channel, as the language used isn't something our team can engage with. Your
> account matter has been passed to a specialist team, and someone will follow up with
> you directly about it. We do want to get your issue resolved.

**CON-004 — request to misstate a claim:**

> I want to get this money back for you if there's a path to it, so let me be straight
> about the one route I can't take: I'm not able to record a claim reason that doesn't
> match what happened. What I can do is file this under [correct reason], which fits your
> situation as you've described it, and get it in front of the team today. Would you like
> me to go ahead with that?

**CON-005 — third-party access:**

> I understand why you're asking, and I'm sorry to be unhelpful here — I'm not able to
> discuss or provide access to an account you're not an owner of, in either direction.
> If you and [the other person] want shared access going forward, a branch appointment
> can set that up with both of you present, usually in a single visit. I'm happy to help
> with anything on your own accounts in the meantime.

**CON-008 — inducement:**

> That's a kind thought, and I appreciate it — but I can't accept anything for helping
> you, and it genuinely won't change how quickly I work on this. Let's get back to it.

**CON-002 — self-harm indicator (specialist handoff, keep short):**

> I hear you, and I don't want to hand you a form letter. I've asked a specialist on our
> team to take this over personally, and they'll be in touch shortly. If you're in
> crisis, you can reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988 (US),
> any time.

## 6. Decision quick reference

| Customer message | Route | Cite |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This is absolute garbage, refund my $35 fee" (first fee, 5 days old) | **Auto-resolve** the fee + one boundary line | CON-001, FEE-001 |
| "You people are idiots" + a valid dispute | **Auto-resolve/escalate on the merits**, never refuse | CON-001, CON-011 |
| "I'll be waiting outside your Elm St branch" | **Escalate — Threat Response, urgent** | CON-002 |
| "I've lost everything, I don't see a point anymore" | **Escalate — Vulnerable Customer Support, urgent** | CON-002 |
| Slur directed at an agent | **Refuse** (scripted) + escalate Conduct Review | CON-003 |
| "Just mark it as fraud, it's easier" | **Refuse**, offer correct claim reason | CON-004 |
| "Read me my wife's transactions" | **Refuse** | CON-005 |
| "I need to track my girlfriend's spending, don't tell her" | **Refuse** + escalate Conduct Review | CON-005 |
| "What dollar amount triggers your fraud review?" | **Refuse** | CON-006 |
| "How do I deposit $30k without it being reported?" | **Refuse** + escalate BSA/AML, no tip-off | CON-007 |
| "I'll Venmo you $50 if you push this through" | **Refuse** | CON-008 |
| "My attorney is filing with the CFPB tomorrow" | **Escalate — Executive Complaints**, do not argue | CON-009 |
| "I'm closing every account, you're the worst bank alive" | Service the closure normally | CON-011, SUB-002 |
